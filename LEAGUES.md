# AETHER leagues and diamond tiers

Monthly imported diamonds determine these tiers and leagues. A boundary is inclusive.

| Tier | Minimum diamonds | League |
|---|---:|---|
| 1 | 0 | Rising |
| 2 | 100,000 | Star |
| 3 | 200,000 | Star |
| 4 | 300,000 | Premier |
| 5 | 500,000 | Elite |
| 6 | 700,000 | Elite |
| 7 | 1,000,000 | Millionaire |
| 8 | 1,600,000 | Millionaire |
| 9 | 2,500,000 | Icon |
| 10 | 5,000,000 | Icon |

There is no tier 0. League changes occur at 100k, 300k, 500k, 1m and 2.5m.
Incentive tiers, referral reward tiers and L0?L5 activity levels are separate
systems and retain their existing rules. Website verified leagues require a
complete calendar-month report; partial-month progress is not a confirmed award.
Use the supplied Rising, Star, Premier, Elite, Millionaire and Icon PNG badges.
The Support Bot and FT Notifier have no independent league classifier.
